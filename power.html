Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked

How long does a solar installation take?
+
Residential installs typically complete in 1–3 days. Commercial projects 1–2 weeks. Industrial projects 2–4 weeks. We provide a detailed project schedule before mobilization.
Do you offer maintenance contracts?
+
Yes. Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC) are available for all installations. AMC includes scheduled inspections, emergency call-outs, parts at discount, and 24/7 remote monitoring access.
Which solar panel brands do you supply?
+
We are authorized distributors of Jinko Solar, LONGi, Canadian Solar, and REC. Inverters from Huawei, SMA, and Growatt. All products carry manufacturer warranties and international certifications.
Can you handle government and large commercial projects?
+
Absolutely. PS Power is registered with BPDB, DESCO, and SREDA — making us eligible for government tenders. We've delivered projects for factories, hospitals, government offices, and large commercial buildings.
Is the site assessment really free?
+
Yes. We offer a free on-site technical assessment for solar and large electrical projects within Bangladesh. A certified engineer visits, analyzes your requirements, and provides a detailed feasibility report at no cost.
What financing options are available?
+
We accept bank transfer, bKash, Nagad, and offer installment plans for qualifying projects. We can also assist with bank financing arrangements for larger commercial and industrial projects.
